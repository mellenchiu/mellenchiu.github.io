研究聚焦於「知識驅動創新」：從跨組織的供應鏈開放式創新、組織內部知識擴散與吸收能力，延伸至生成式 AI 於高等教育之使用者覺察、信任與學習韌性，並將研究成果轉化為 AI 應用、數據分析與智慧餐飲之教學實踐。

Research Focus

研究主軸：知識驅動創新Research Agenda: Knowledge-Driven Innovation

研究脈絡由「跨組織知識協作 → 組織內部創新機制 → 個人對新興科技的認知與採用」逐層深化，呈現「深化 → 卓越 → 前瞻」的發展軌跡。

The agenda moves from inter-organizational knowledge collaboration, to intra-organizational innovation mechanisms, to individual cognition and adoption of emerging technologies — a trajectory of deepening, excellence and foresight.

核心：知識如何驅動創新與學習Core question: how knowledge drives innovation and learning
主軸一｜跨組織Stream 1 · Inter-organizational

供應鏈協作與開放式創新Supply-chain collaboration & open innovation

以社會資本、正義理論與治理機制，探討供應鏈夥伴如何共享知識、建立開放式創新能力；發現 IoT 與雲端等科技基礎比正式契約更能推動創新。

Uses social capital, justice and governance perspectives to explain how supply-chain partners share knowledge and build open innovation capability — showing that IoT and cloud technology foundations drive innovation more than formal contracts.

IJIM 2018JIK 2022JETM 2015
主軸二｜組織內Stream 2 · Intra-organizational

知識擴散、吸收能力與社群媒體Knowledge diffusion, absorptive capacity & social media

整合內外在動機、TOE 架構與媒體依賴理論，建構跨層次模型，說明社群媒體知識內化與個人吸收能力如何強化內向型開放創新。

Integrates intrinsic/extrinsic motivation, the TOE framework and media dependency theory in multi-level models showing how social-media knowledge internalization and individual absorptive capacity strengthen inbound open innovation.

結合現狀偏誤理論與科技可供性理論，探討學生對 GAI 的覺察、信任與知覺有用性，並延伸至學習韌性與 GAI 輔助教學框架。

Combines status-quo-bias and technology-affordance theories to study students' awareness, trust and perceived usefulness of GenAI, extending to learning resilience and GenAI-assisted teaching frameworks.

Aligning tourists' needs with loyalty programs: A framework for enhancing brand loyalty

契合遊客需求的忠誠度方案：提升品牌忠誠度之架構

Chiu, M.-L., & Lin, C.-N. · Journal of Destination Marketing & Management, 101129

摘要（中譯）｜本研究探討觀光與餐旅產業的忠誠度方案，如何在經濟誘因與情感滿足、社交參與等內在回饋之間取得平衡，並將社群媒體納入作為顧客互動的工具。本研究以問卷調查法蒐集 571 位受試者資料。研究發現，同時滿足內在需求與財務誘因的忠誠度方案，能帶來更強的顧客忠誠；社群媒體互動提升了方案成效，產品涉入則具有調節作用。研究建議管理者可在忠誠度方案設計中整合體驗與社交元素，以強化顧客關係並降低財務風險。

Abstract · This study explores how loyalty programs in the tourism and hospitality industry could balance economic incentives with intrinsic rewards such as emotional satisfaction and social engagement while incorporating social media as a tool for customer engagement. Data was collected from 571 participants using the survey method. The findings indicate that loyalty programs addressing both intrinsic needs and financial incentives contribute to stronger customer loyalty. Social media engagement enhanced the effectiveness of these programs, while product involvement served as a moderating factor. The findings suggest that managers can foster stronger customer relationships and mitigate financial risks by integrating experiential and social elements into loyalty program design.

Exploring user awareness and perceived usefulness of generative AI in higher education: The moderating role of trust

探討高等教育中生成式 AI 的使用者覺察與知覺有用性：信任的調節角色

Chiu, M.-L.* · Education and Information Technologies

摘要（中譯）｜生成式人工智慧（GAI）以其獨特能力，正為多個產業帶來深刻變革。在以人類智識與教學發展為核心的高等教育領域，這些新興科技正處於關鍵的轉變時刻。在 GAI 的範疇中，人與科技互動的動態深受使用者覺察的影響，其涵蓋對科技特性的理解與使用經驗。本研究以現狀偏誤（SQB）理論為視角，探討高等教育導入 GAI 的影響，聚焦於使用者覺察如何影響知覺效用。研究採量化方法，以偏最小平方結構方程模型（PLS-SEM）檢驗影響教育場域 GAI 導入的各項因素之關係。結果顯示，使用者覺察顯著受到兩大構面影響：個人抗拒承諾與科技可供性。在個人抗拒承諾構面中，沉沒成本與隱私顧慮皆對使用者覺察有顯著影響；在科技可供性構面中，可解釋性、問責性與透明度對使用者覺察具顯著正向效果。研究結果為導入 GAI 的高等教育機構提供重要洞見，指出成功導入需同時考量個人抗拒因素與科技可供性特徵，並有助於理解教育情境中的 GAI 採用，為擬定有效導入策略提供實務意涵。

Abstract · Generative Artificial Intelligence (GAI), with its distinctive capabilities, is inducing profound transformations across multiple industries. In the realm of higher education, a centered around human intellect and pedagogical advancement, these emerging technologies are at a pivotal juncture of change. Within the scope of GAI, the dynamics of human-technology interaction are significantly influenced by user cognizance, encompassing an understanding and experiential knowledge of technological features. This study investigates the impact of GAI adoption in higher education, focusing on how user awareness influences perceived utility through the lens of the Status Quo Bias (SQB) theory. The research employs a quantitative approach using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modelling (PLS-SEM) to examine the relationships between various factors affecting GAI implementation in educational settings. The findings reveal significant effects on user awareness from two main dimensions: individual commitment to resistance and technology affordance. In the individual commitment to resistance dimension, both sunk cost and privacy concerns demonstrated significant influences on user awareness. Within the technology affordance dimension, explainability, accountability, and transparency showed significant positive effects on user awareness. These results provide valuable insights for higher education institutions implementing GAI technologies, suggesting that successful adoption requires careful consideration of both individual resistance factors and technological affordance features. The findings contribute to the understanding of GAI adoption in educational contexts and offer practical implications for developing effective implementation strategies.

Driving open innovation capability through new knowledge diffusion of integrating intrinsic and extrinsic motivations in organizations: Moderator of individual absorptive capacity

整合內外在動機之新知識擴散驅動組織開放式創新能力：個人吸收能力的調節效果

Chiu, M.-L., Cheng, T.-S., & Lin, C.-N. · Journal of the Knowledge Economy, 15(1), 3685–3717

摘要（中譯）｜組織內的開放式創新著重於新知識在不同部門員工之間的傳播。當員工與同事分享知識的意願提高，新知識便得以擴散，因此了解哪些因素影響組織內的知識流動至關重要。人類行為深受內在與外在動機影響：科技－組織－環境（TOE）架構可視為與分享行為及新知識擴散相關的外在動機；此外，個人具有支持知識交流的先天心理需求，使其樂於與他人互動。本研究以內外在動機及 TOE 模型為基礎，提出一個創新且完整的研究模型，探討影響組織開放式創新能力的因素。研究自 600 家組織回收 217 份問卷，並以 PLS 驗證模型。實證結果顯示：（1）組織文化、破壞性科技等外在動機具重要且強烈的影響，互動渴望等內在動機亦不容忽視；（2）新知識擴散對開放式創新能力有顯著影響；（3）個人吸收能力的調節作用可強化新知識擴散與開放式創新能力之間的關聯。研究結果加深了對「何種動機驅動新知識擴散以傳遞組織開放式創新能力」的理解。

Abstract · Open innovation within an organization focuses on the dissemination of new knowledge among employees in different departmental units. When the willingness to share knowledge with colleagues increases, new knowledge dissemination can be achieved. Therefore, it is vital to understand which factors can influence knowledge flow in an organization. Human behavior is strongly influenced by internal and external motivations. The technology-organization-environment (TOE) framework can be considered an extrinsic motivation associated with the sharing behavior and the diffusion of new knowledge. Furthermore, individuals have innate psychological needs that support knowledge exchange, making them eager to interact with others. Based on the internal and external motivations and the TOE model, this study proposes an innovative and complete research model to explore the factors which affect open innovation capability within an organization. There were 217 responses to this research questionnaire from 600 organizations, and PLS was used to validate the model. Empirical findings indicate: (1) the importance of extrinsic motivation such as organizational culture and disruptive technology, having strong effects as intrinsic motivation like interactive desire is also concerned; (2) new knowledge diffusion has a significant effect on open innovation capability; (3) the moderating factor of individual absorptive capacity may strengthen the connection between new knowledge diffusion and open innovation capability. The results increase our understanding for what motivations actually drive new knowledge diffusion in transmitting open innovation capability in organizations.

The mediating role of absorptive capability between the effect of organizational internalization through social media on open inbound innovation
